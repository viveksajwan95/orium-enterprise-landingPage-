Common questions

Questions, answered.

What is Orium Enterprises?

Orium Enterprises is an advisory-led enterprise that owns and operates two companies — Orium Packaging, a food packaging manufacturer, and Orium Technology, an AI-first technology consultancy. The group sets one standard both companies answer to.

Where does the name Orium come from?

Orium is derived from orichalcum — the legendary metal of Atlantis, described by Plato as glowing like fire and more precious than gold. The name carries the same promise: a standard forged to endure.

Can the group combine packaging and technology in one program?

Yes. The group's advisory layer designs integrated programs — smart packaging, digital traceability and supply-chain automation — where the packaging line and the technology stack are delivered as one project with one accountable partner.

Who runs the enterprise?

A senior team across both companies — engineers, operators and specialists spread across North America and Europe. No layers between you and the people doing the work, and one standard every company answers to.
